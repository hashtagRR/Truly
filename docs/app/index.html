      Privacy

      • Your check-ins are stored only in this browser on this device.
      • The camera is off until you start a camera check, and it switches off by itself after a few seconds.
      • Camera frames are read into memory, scored on this device and then discarded. No image is ever saved or sent.
      • To show helplines near you, Truly reads your device's time zone. It never uses your location or IP address, and nothing is sent anywhere. If you choose a country yourself, that choice is kept in this browser.
      • Truly has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no tracking.
