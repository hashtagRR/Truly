Truly

How are you, truly?

Some days you smile for everyone and still feel heavy inside. Truly asks how you really are, then uses music the way music therapists do. It meets your mood first and gently lifts it, one song at a time. When you need more, it helps you reach someone.

How it works

Four small steps. Your own answers always come first.

  1. Step 1

    Ask

    A short check-in asks how you feel and how much energy you have. It takes under a minute.

  2. Step 2

    Notice

    If you turn it on, the camera notices when the face you show is different from how you say you feel.

  3. Step 3

    Meet, then move

    Following a music therapy method called the iso principle, songs start close to how you feel, then shift a little at a time towards calmer or brighter.

  4. Step 4

    Reach out

    When low days add up, Truly gently suggests talking to someone and shows helplines where you live.

Why it works this way

Every choice in Truly comes from published research.

  • Your words come first

    Short questionnaires are one of the most trusted ways to understand low mood. So Truly starts by asking.

    Kroenke and colleagues, 2001

  • A smile can hide a lot

    Faces do not reliably show how someone feels. That is why the camera never decides anything.

    Barrett and colleagues, 2019

  • Your voice says more

    Pauses and tone change with low mood. Truly can listen to how you speak, never to what you say.

    Cummins and colleagues, 2015; Briganti and Lechien, 2025

  • Music can lift, step by step

    Music therapy eases depression in the short term. Meeting your mood first, then moving gently, is how therapists use it.

    Aalbers and colleagues, 2017; Starcke and von Georgi, 2024

Private by design

  • Everything runs on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • Camera and voice are off until you turn them on, and they are never recorded or stored.
  • The voice check listens to how you speak, never to the words.
  • Your check-ins stay on your device, and you can delete them at any time.
  • Truly never diagnoses you or puts a label on you. It is a companion, not a medical device.

Open for everyone

Truly is free software, built on openly shared research, data and models. Developers, researchers and music therapists are welcome to help, from the music catalogue to helplines for another country.
