# Music catalogue source

`catalogue.json` lists the lyric-free pieces from [MCUD 1.0](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.30517820.v7) (Du et al., 2026): title, artist, valence, arousal, tempo and the public source link used in each trial. MCUD 1.0 is licensed under CC BY 4.0, and the catalogue is shared under the same licence with credit to its authors. The full citation is in [references/DATASETS.md](https://github.com/hashtagRR/Truly/blob/main/references/DATASETS.md).
